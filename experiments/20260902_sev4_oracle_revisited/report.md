# The severity four bound, retested with four evidence functions and confirmed nested

The recorded bound said the true site loses the correlation on 66 percent of
this generator's severity four pairs even with the true pose supplied. That
measurement predated the raw full reference statistic and had never faced the
matched filter, so the same 32 pairs were probed again at the true pose under
four statistics: the shipped bandpassed bank template, the raw formation
statistic the released gate uses, the bandpassed template widened to cover
the whole reference, and Wiener weighted correlation, the matched filter
under the measured noise floor that the literature names as the principled
replacement for a fixed bandpass.

    statistic at the true pose      true site wins
    shipped bandpassed template     11 of 32   34 percent
    raw full reference formation     4 of 32   12 percent
    full coverage bandpassed        10 of 32   31 percent
    Wiener weighted, matched filter  9 of 32   28 percent
    any of the four                 11 of 32   34 percent

The union line is the result. Every pair any alternative wins, the shipped
statistic already wins: the four win sets are nested inside the shipped one,
so there is no complementary evidence to ensemble and the matched filter
recovers zero additional pairs. Together with the factor attribution that
puts 65 percent of the severity four loss on shot noise and beam blur,
photons never collected, the bound now stands on four independent statistics
rather than one, and the found flag remains the correct response to the
regime, established rather than assumed.

Scope, which the headline sentence previously blurred: this ceiling belongs
to this repository's own generator, whose severity four runs at roughly a
third of the released recipe's dose with search only defocus and differential
polygon scaling the released pipeline never renders. On the released physics
the same build earns 0.85 to 1.00 severity four credit, five of five pairs at
full credit on the hardened battery, so the regime the bound describes is one
the blind set is not expected to enter. The raw statistic's 12 percent here
against its severity four rescues there is the same fact from the other side:
that statistic is powerful exactly where the generator guarantees its
formation, and this generator does not.
